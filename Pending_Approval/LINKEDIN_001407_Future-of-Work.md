# LinkedIn Post Draft: Future of Work

## Post Content

Hot take: most businesses don't need more tools. They need fewer tools that actually work together.

I've been thinking a lot about future of work lately.

Here's what I've learned after building AI-powered workflows for real businesses:

1. Start with the pain, not the tech. Ask "what's costing us time?" before "what AI should we use?"

2. Keep humans in the loop. The best AI systems augment human judgment — they don't replace it.

3. Measure what matters. If your automation doesn't save measurable time or money, it's just a demo.

What's your take? Are you seeing AI actually deliver ROI, or is it still mostly hype in your industry?
